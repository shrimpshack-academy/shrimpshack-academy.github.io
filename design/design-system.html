Design rules

Phone firstTap targets at least 44px, one task per screen, bottom navigation on phones.
3 languagesEnglish (left-to-right), Arabic and Urdu (right-to-left). Arrows and layout mirror automatically.
ThemesLight by default. The dark switch sits next to the language buttons.
Brand motifYellow wave line from the menu under headers and course covers, used sparingly.
Corners and shadowsRounded 14px cards, soft shadows, warm paper background.
